Rostas Coordinator Guide

Fair, automatic, kind rostering for churches and volunteer organisations.

What is Rostas?

Rostas is your organisation's volunteer roster assistant. It handles the time-consuming, people-sensitive work of building rosters, collecting availability, and finding replacements when someone can't make it — automatically, fairly, and kindly.

Once set up, Rostas runs most of the roster cycle on its own. Your job shifts from juggling spreadsheets and chasing people by text to simply reviewing what Rostas has prepared and pressing go.

The old wayThe Rostas way
2–3 hours of emails, calls, and chasingUnder 1 hour via a guided 6-step wizard
Flat yes/no availability guesswork3-state availability + 4-tier role preferences
Coordinator manually calls around when someone cancelsAutomated SMS replacement chain handles it automatically
High risk of volunteer burnout from over-rosteringFrequency caps and self-serve opt-outs built in

How Rostas Works — The Big Picture

Rostas works in a rhythm that repeats every few weeks. The six-step Run Sheet Wizard guides you through each stage in order:

StepNameWhat happens
1Refresh & SyncRostas refreshes your seasons calendar, overlays public and school holidays, and confirms your volunteer list is up to date.
2Role PrefsVolunteers set which roles they're willing to perform — Primary, Fillable, or Not for me. Set once, remembered for every future build.
3AvailabilityRostas emails volunteers asking which upcoming dates they're available. Volunteers tap a link — no login, no app to download.
4Build RosterThe engine automatically fills every slot using a four-tier priority system. The build takes about 30 seconds.
5Check RosterYou review what Rostas has built using the traffic light system — green filled, amber pending, red unfilled.
6NotifyRostas emails every rostered volunteer their assignment. Automatic reminders follow closer to the date — no further action needed.

Key Concepts

Availability — Simple for Volunteers

Before each build, Rostas emails your volunteers asking which upcoming dates they're available. Volunteers receive a personalised link — no password, no app — and see a simple calendar of dates. Everything starts as Available — volunteers only change the dates they can't make.

✅ Available
The default. Volunteer is free and happy to serve on this date.
❓ Not sure
Volunteer might be available. Rostas may provisionally assign them.
❌ Can't make it
Volunteer is unavailable. They won't be assigned to this date.
💡 If a volunteer doesn't respond, Rostas sends an automatic SMS nudge after a configurable number of days. No manual chasing needed.
The Intelligent Matching Engine

The roster engine doesn't fill slots blindly. It uses a four-tier priority system that respects both when volunteers can serve and what they actually want to do:

TierAvailabilityRole matchPriority
1✅ AvailablePrimary role (Yes, I do this)Highest — assigned first
2✅ AvailableFillable role (Happy to fill in)Second choice
3❓ Not surePrimary roleThird — provisional
4❓ Not sureFillable roleLast resort

Within each tier, Rostas selects the volunteer who has been rostered least recently — keeping things fair automatically over time without any manual tracking.

The Roster Board

The Board is your day-to-day view of the current roster. It shows every slot, every date, and every volunteer assignment at a glance. A traffic light colour system tells you instantly what's filled, what's pending, and what needs attention.

🟢 Filled
Volunteer confirmed. No action needed.
🟡 Pending
Provisionally assigned (volunteer marked Not sure). Monitor for confirmation.
🔴 Unfilled
No suitable volunteer found automatically. Needs your attention.

The Automated SMS Replacement Chain

When a rostered volunteer can't make it, they tap a link in their notification email. Rostas immediately starts working through the backup list — no coordinator involvement needed.

🌙 Quiet hours are always respected. Rostas never sends automated SMS between 9pm and 8am. If the replacement chain is running overnight, it pauses and resumes in the morning automatically.

Looking After Your Volunteers

Rostas is designed with your volunteers in mind, not just your roster.

FeatureWhat it does for your volunteers
Frequency capsSet a maximum number of times each volunteer can be rostered per month. The engine never exceeds this, even if they're always available.
Quiet hoursNo automated SMS between 9pm and 8am. Volunteers are never disturbed at night.
Self-serve opt-outVolunteers can pause or opt out of SMS and email at any time using a link in any Rostas message — no need to contact you.
Relational rosteringRostas tries to schedule family members and preferred buddies on the same dates — making it easier to serve together.

Who Does What

Rostas is designed so that most of the routine work happens automatically. Here's how responsibilities divide between you, your volunteers, and Rostas:

WhoWhat they do
You — the coordinatorRun the 6-step wizard each cycle, review the built roster, handle exceptions, manage volunteers and settings
Your volunteersRespond to availability emails, set role preferences once, accept or decline replacement requests by tapping a link
RostasEverything in between — building the roster, sending notifications, chasing replacements, sending reminders, enforcing frequency caps
✨ Rostas doesn't replace your judgement. There will be times when you want to override what Rostas has built — swap two people, add someone manually, or put a slot on hold. The Board makes all of this easy. Rostas handles the routine so you can focus on the exceptions.

Part B Setting Up Rostas First-run setup wizard · Adding volunteers · Your first roster type

When you open Rostas for the first time, the setup wizard runs automatically. It takes about five minutes and covers the essentials — your organisation name, your contact details, and a quick SMS test to make sure everything is working before your first roster build.

The wizard runs once. After it's complete, Rostas opens directly to the Build Dashboard on every future visit.

ℹ️ Before you start: Your Rostas coordinator needs to have set up the system and given you the link to open it. If you don't have a link yet, ask them for it.

The Setup Wizard

The wizard has four steps shown across the top of the screen. You can see at a glance how far through you are and what's coming next.

Step 1 — Your Organisation

The first step establishes your organisation's identity. The name you enter here appears in every message sent to volunteers — availability emails, roster notifications, and SMS reminders.

FieldWhat to enterRequired?
Organisation nameYour organisation's full name as you want it to appear to volunteers — e.g. "Riverside Community Church". Maximum 40 characters.Yes
TimezoneSelect your timezone from the dropdown. This controls all scheduling and quiet hours.Yes
Country / calling codeSelect your country. This is used to format volunteer phone numbers correctly for SMS delivery.Yes
WebsiteOptional. Your organisation's website URL — must start with https://No

Click Next → when all required fields are filled. The button stays disabled until everything is valid.

Step 2 — Your Details

Step 2 captures the primary coordinator's contact details. These are used for SMS test sends, coordinator escalation alerts when the replacement chain is exhausted, and the top bar of the coordinator app.

FieldWhat to enter
Your nameYour full name as coordinator — e.g. "Sarah Mitchell"
Your emailYour email address — receives error alerts and escalation notifications
Your mobileYour mobile number in full international format — e.g. +6421123456. Must include country code.
⚠️ Include your country code in the mobile number. Enter it as +6421123456, not 021123456. Without the country code SMS delivery will fail.

Step 3 — SMS Test

Before your first roster build, Rostas verifies that SMS is working. This step sends a real test message to your mobile so you can confirm everything is set up correctly.

How to complete Step 3

Your mobile number is pre-filled from Step 2. Tap Send test SMS → and wait for the message to arrive on your phone — usually within 30 seconds. Once received, a green confirmation appears and the Next button enables.

💡 If the test message doesn't arrive within a minute, check that your mobile number includes the country code and is entered correctly. You can edit the number in the field and try again. If it still fails, contact your tech support person — the SMS connection may need to be configured.

Step 4 — Done

Once SMS is confirmed, the wizard completes and Rostas is ready to use.

From here you have two options:

  • Go to Build → — takes you straight to the Build Dashboard, ready to start your first roster cycle. This is the right choice if your volunteers are already imported.
  • Explore Settings — opens the Settings view if you want to review or customise anything before your first build.

Adding Your Volunteers

Before you can build a roster, Rostas needs to know who your volunteers are. There are two ways to get them in:

MethodBest forWhere to find it
CSV ImportMost organisations — import from a spreadsheet you already haveVolunteers → Import CSV
Add manuallySmall teams or adding individuals one at a timeVolunteers → Add Volunteer

Importing from CSV

If you have a spreadsheet of volunteers — from a previous roster system, a church database, or even a simple contact list — you can import them directly using the CSV Import Wizard. The wizard maps your spreadsheet columns to the right fields in Rostas and handles phone number formatting automatically.

To import volunteers from a spreadsheet

Go to Volunteers in the sidebar, then click Import CSV. The wizard walks you through uploading your file, mapping columns, and reviewing the results before importing. Phone numbers are automatically normalised to the correct international format for SMS delivery.

💡 Your CSV needs at minimum: volunteer name and mobile number. Email address is strongly recommended so volunteers can receive roster notifications by email. Any additional columns can be mapped or skipped during the import.

Adding Volunteers Manually

To add a single volunteer, go to Volunteers in the sidebar and click Add Volunteer. Fill in their name, mobile, and email, then save. They'll appear in the volunteers list immediately and can be included in your next roster build.

What's Next

Once your volunteers are in, you're ready to build your first roster. Head to Part C — The Roster Cycle which walks you through the six-step wizard from start to finish.

📋 Setup checklist before your first build:
  • ✓ Organisation name set
  • ✓ Coordinator contact details set
  • ✓ SMS test confirmed working
  • ✓ Volunteers imported or added
  • ○ Volunteer role preferences set (can be done during the wizard)
Part C The Roster Cycle New Roster Wizard · Rebuild Wizard · Building and sending your roster

There are two roster wizards in Rostas. The New Roster Wizard runs once when you set up a new roster type — it defines the name, roles, season dates, and volunteers. The Rebuild Wizard runs every few weeks to collect availability, build the roster, and send notifications to volunteers.

Both start from the Build Dashboard.

The Build Dashboard

The Build Dashboard is your home base for all roster activity. It shows every active roster type with its current status and coverage bar at a glance.

Status badgeWhat it means
ActiveNotifications sent. Roster is live for the current cycle.
Ready to SendRoster built and reviewed — ready to send notifications.
In ProgressA build is partially complete — some steps still to do.
Season ConfirmNext season needs to be confirmed before a new build can start.
💡 New Roster — the green card at the bottom of the dashboard. Click it to set up a brand new roster type from scratch.

The New Roster Wizard

Run this wizard when you're setting up a roster type for the first time — for example, adding a Music roster or a Hospitality team. It takes about 10 minutes and walks you through five steps: Name & Icon, Roles, Season, Volunteers, and Review.

ℹ️ You only run the New Roster Wizard once per roster type. After setup is complete, all future roster builds use the Rebuild Wizard instead.

Step 1 — Name & Icon

Give your roster a short name and choose an icon. The name appears in SMS messages, emails, and the coordinator app — keep it brief and clear.

Step 2 — Roles

Define the roles that need to be filled each time this roster runs. For each role, set a minimum and maximum number of people required.

💡 Role names matter. Use clear, specific names that volunteers will recognise — e.g. "Sound Desk", "Welcome Team", "Data Projection". These names appear in SMS messages and emails sent to volunteers.

Step 3 — Season

A season defines the date range, frequency, and day of week for this roster. Rostas uses seasons to calculate which dates to roster for, and to overlay public and school holidays automatically.

FieldWhat to enter
Start dateThe first date this roster will run
End dateThe last date of this season — typically end of term, semester, or year
FrequencyWeekly or Fortnightly
Day of weekWhich day the roster runs — e.g. Sunday, Monday
Pause school holidaysTick if this roster doesn't run during school holidays
Pause public holidaysTick if this roster doesn't run on public holidays

Step 4 — Volunteers

Select which volunteers from your list will serve on this roster. Only volunteers already in Rostas can be added here — if someone is missing, add them in the Volunteers section first then return to this step.

⚠️ Screenshot coming — this section will be updated with a screenshot of the volunteer selection step shortly.

Step 5 — Review

The final step shows a summary of everything you've set up — roster name, roles, season dates, and volunteer count. Review it carefully before confirming.

Click Confirm & Create Roster to finish. Your new roster type appears on the Build Dashboard immediately. You're now ready to run your first Rebuild Wizard for this roster.

The Rebuild Wizard

The Rebuild Wizard is the recurring roster cycle you run every few weeks. It collects availability from volunteers, builds the roster automatically, lets you review it, and sends notifications — all in six guided steps.

To start, click any roster card on the Build Dashboard. If a build is already in progress, you'll be taken straight to where you left off.

Step 1 — Refresh & Sync

The first step refreshes your seasons calendar — overlaying public and school holidays — and confirms your volunteer list is current. This takes about 30 seconds and happens automatically when you start.

⚠️ Screenshot coming — wizard steps 0–3 screenshots will be added shortly.
💡 Always run this step at the start of each new cycle. It ensures your dates are correct before availability emails go out.

Step 2 — Role Preferences

This step gives volunteers the opportunity to update their role preferences — which roles they're willing to perform. You can either send a role preference email to all volunteers, or skip this step if preferences are already up to date.

When to send role preference emails

Send them at the start of a new season, when new volunteers have joined, or when you know someone's availability for certain roles has changed. For a routine rebuild mid-season, it's usually safe to skip this step.

Step 3 — Availability

Rostas emails all active volunteers asking which upcoming dates they're available. Each volunteer receives a personalised link — no login required. They tap their dates, mark any they can't make, and save.

⏰ Allow 5–7 days for responses before moving to Step 4. Volunteers who don't respond within a configurable number of days automatically receive an SMS nudge.

Step 4 — Build Roster

With one click, the roster engine analyses every volunteer — their availability, role preferences, how recently they've served, and their monthly frequency cap — and fills every slot automatically. The build takes about 30 seconds for most rosters.

⚙️ The engine uses a four-tier priority system — see Part A, The Intelligent Matching Engine for full detail on how slots are filled.

Step 5 — Check Roster

Before anything is sent to volunteers, you review what Rostas has built. The Communications Preview shows you exactly what every volunteer will receive — the email, the SMS, the roster view — before a single message leaves the system.

🟢 Filled
Volunteer confirmed. Notification ready to send.
🟡 Pending
Provisionally assigned (volunteer marked Not sure). Will receive a confirm/decline message.
🔴 Unfilled
No volunteer found. Address before sending if possible.
⚠️ Don't skip this step. It's your last chance to catch problems before volunteers are notified. Check for unfilled slots, confirm the message wording looks right, and make any manual swaps needed.

Step 6 — Notify

Once you're satisfied with the roster, click Send Notifications. Every rostered volunteer receives a personalised email with their assignments and a "Can't make it?" link. An SMS is sent at the same time.

📬 What happens automatically after Step 6:
  • Email reminder sent 3 days before each service date
  • SMS reminder sent 1 day before each service date
  • If a volunteer taps "Can't make it" — the SMS replacement chain starts automatically
No further action needed until the next roster cycle.

Confirming a New Season

When the current season is coming to an end, the Build Dashboard shows a Season Confirm button on the relevant roster card. You need to confirm the next season's dates before you can run a new build.

To confirm a new season

Click the Season Confirm button on the Build Dashboard card. Review the proposed start and end dates, adjust if needed, then click Confirm Season. The roster card updates and you can proceed with a new build.

💡 Confirm the season early — ideally a few weeks before the current season ends, so you have time to collect availability and build the next roster before the first date arrives.
